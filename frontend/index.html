Electives — paste from your sheet

One subject per line: group, code, subject name, faculty, second faculty (optional), separated by tabs (copy straight from a sheet) or 2+ spaces. Use - for no second faculty. Subjects of the same group (E1, E2 …) run at the same time and share the group's 12 weekly hours (2 a day). Faculty already in the list are matched by name; any missing ones are added.

Blocked periods

Periods no class can be scheduled in, for example a weekly meeting or mentoring hour. Choose whether a block applies to every semester or only one.
